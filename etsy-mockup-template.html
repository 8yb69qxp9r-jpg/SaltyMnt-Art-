How to set this up (one-time) Click to collapse
  1. Scene photos. Grab a few empty-wall room photos you can use commercially: Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay. On each card, click Upload scene photo.
  2. Photo-real frames (optional). If you want true photo frames instead of the built-in styled ones, grab transparent-center frame PNGs: PNGWing, CleanPNG, PNGEgg. On each card, set Frame → ✨ Custom photo frame…, upload the PNG, then dial in the four insets so the artwork fills the opening.
  3. Click Edit placement, drag the frame onto the wall, resize/rotate, click Save.
  4. Drop your artwork in the box at top. From now on, just swap artwork and download — placements stick (saved in this browser).

Tip: pick scene photos shot straight-on, and frame PNGs with a clean transparent rectangle for the opening.

Pick the ratio your file was designed at. Frames match it automatically — no off-center crops.

Contain = full art visible (best for digital prints). Cover = fills frame, may crop.
