# Pin Copy Formulas

Per-collection title and description patterns. Written once per master, reused across all
8 of that master's layouts.

## Hard limits

| Field  | Limit | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| `title` | ≤100 chars | Pinterest truncates ~40 in feed — front-load the subject |
| `desc`  | 150–300 chars | Below 150 reads thin; above 300 gets clipped |
| `tags`  | 3–6 phrases | Reference only; usually not entered in the form |

No hashtags. No emoji. No ALL CAPS. No "BUY NOW" / "SHOP NOW!!" — the nudge is soft.

## Title formula

```
[Specific Subject] [Art Form] — [Style or Distinguishing Detail]
```

The subject is a real named place, peak, or verse reference. The style detail is what
makes this print different from the other 34.

- ✅ `Acadia National Park Wall Art — Vintage WPA Poster`
- ✅ `Mount Elbert Print, 14,433 ft — Black & White Summit View`
- ❌ `Beautiful Mountain Print for Your Home` — no place, no style, no reason to click
- ❌ `SALTY MOUNTAIN DIGITAL | Wall Art | Printable | Instant Download` — brand-first,
  keyword-stuffed, tells the pinner nothing

Brand goes in the wordmark on the image, not at the front of the title.

## Description formula

Three beats, then the nudge:

```
[What you're looking at, concretely] [Why it works on a wall / what mood it sets]
[Format + delivery]. [Soft Etsy nudge.]
```

Worked example (C3, 268 chars):

> Acadia's rugged Maine coast at first light, rendered as a vintage WPA-style travel
> poster. Warm ochre and deep spruce tones that settle a hallway or study without
> shouting. High-resolution printable in five standard sizes — instant download, print
> at home or at a local shop. Find it on Etsy.

Nudge options, rotate them: `Find it on Etsy.` · `Instant download on Etsy.` ·
`Printable — available on Etsy.` · `On Etsy as an instant download.`

## Per-collection voice

### C1 — Atmospheric Landscapes → `Atmospheric Landscape Wall Art` / `Moody Mountain & Coastal Prints`
Quiet, weather-forward, unpeopled. Lead with light and air — fog, first light, low cloud.
Name the range or coast. Avoid "breathtaking," "stunning," "majestic."

> Low fog holding in the Sawatch at dawn, shot in pure neutral black and white…

### C2 — Scripture & Faith → `Scripture Wall Art — Cinematic`
Reverent, never saccharine. The verse reference is required in the title. **The verse text
must come from the image and the tracker — never write one from memory, and never
paraphrase.** Flag every C2 row for Chad's sign-off before it reaches the scheduler.

> Psalm 121:1 set against a cinematic mountain horizon…

Title pattern: `[Verse Ref] Wall Art — [Scene], [Translation]`

### C3 — Vintage Americana → `Vintage National Park & Americana Posters`
Period-accurate, warm, a little nostalgic. WPA / mid-century travel-poster register. Name
the park. Reference the palette. Fine to mention the poster tradition explicitly.

### C4 — Gallery Wall Bundles → `Gallery Wall Ideas & Sets`
Practical and set-oriented. Lead with the count and how it hangs. This is the collection
where the pinner is solving a layout problem, so say the arrangement out loud.

> Six-print set for a gallery wall — three across, two down, or a single row above a
> sofa…

Title pattern: `[Count]-Print Gallery Wall Set — [Theme]`

## The AI disclosure

All SMD art is AI-generated and every pin ships with **Mark as AI-Modified = ON** at
schedule time. You don't need to say "AI-generated" in the description — the platform
label covers it. Leaning into process is a deliberate creative choice on specific pins
(the honesty/manifesto layout), not a line to sprinkle everywhere.

## Sanity check before handoff

- Does every title name a real, specific place or verse?
- Would the description make sense to someone who has never heard of the shop?
- Is the same claim repeated across every master? (If all 35 say "settles a room," rewrite —
  the profile reads as a template.)
- C2 rows flagged for verse sign-off?
