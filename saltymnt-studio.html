How the workflow works

  1. Set up a collection. Use the dropdown in the header → + New. Each collection (Mountain, Blueprints, etc.) keeps its own 10 mockups and 10 pins.
  2. Fill the Mockup Library. Open the Mockup Library tab. For each of the 10 slots, upload a scene photo from your Etsy frame bundle, then click Edit placement and drag the blue rectangle to cover the frame opening. Mark one as ★ Hero — it becomes Etsy listing photo #1.
  3. Fill the Pin Library. Same flow on the Pin Library tab — upload your Canva pin templates, set format (2:3 / 1:1 / 2:1), drag the art rectangle into the open area.
  4. Produce. Open Production, drop your Firefly master, type a filename base, pick print sizes, hit the three Download buttons. You get three zips.
  5. Backup. Use Export library to save your collections + all uploaded scene photos as one JSON onto an external drive. Import library restores it.

Filename convention

  • Listing photos: 01_hero_<slot>.png through 10_<slot>.png
  • Prints: <name>_8x10.jpg, <name>_A4.jpg, etc.
  • Pins: <name>_pin_<slot>.png

Storage: collections, scene photos, pin templates, and frame PNGs are kept in your browser via IndexedDB. Use Export library regularly so you have a copy on disk.
